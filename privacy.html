LENDAS ENTERRADAS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Lendas Enterradas ("the app") is a private desktop tool used by the creator of the Lendas Enterradas channel to publish the channel's short videos to its own TikTok account.

2. Data we access

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): TikTok user ID (open_id), display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens: issued by TikTok after you authorize the app, used only to upload and publish videos on your behalf.
  • Videos and captions (scopes video.upload and video.publish): the videos and captions you choose to post, sent directly to TikTok.

We do not access your followers, messages, likes, watch history or any other TikTok data.

3. How data is stored

All data stays on the computer where the app runs. The app has no external server and does not store TikTok data in the cloud.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties. Data is only sent to TikTok, through its official APIs, to perform the actions you request.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and profile data are kept only while the account is connected. You can disconnect in the app at any time, revoke the app's access in your TikTok settings, or ask us by email to delete any stored data.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not offered to the general public.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: lendasenterradas@gmail.com

Resumo em português

O app acessa apenas o perfil básico do TikTok (ID, nome e avatar), os tokens de acesso e os vídeos e legendas que você escolhe publicar. Tudo fica guardado no computador onde o app roda, sem servidor externo. Nenhum dado é vendido ou compartilhado: os dados só vão para o TikTok, pelas APIs oficiais. Você pode desconectar a conta, revogar o acesso nas configurações do TikTok ou pedir a exclusão dos dados por e-mail.
